## About Me

Data Scientist working in financial services, focused on credit risk analytics, predictive modelling, and building data infrastructure that teams can actually trust and use without analyst involvement.

Currently at **GC Business Finance**, where I am building the analytics capability for a responsible lending division from the ground up, covering everything from data foundations and MI reporting through to machine learning and causal inference.

**What I work on:**
- Production ML models for credit risk: arrears prediction, survival analysis, and portfolio loss forecasting
- Causal inference to measure whether interventions actually work, not just whether they correlate with outcomes
- Explainable AI so that every model decision can be explained in plain English to a director or a regulator
- Self-serve Power BI dashboards that run without analyst involvement
- Python and SQL built to production standards: typed, tested, documented, and logged

**Background:**
- MSc Data Science, Liverpool John Moores University (2022)
- BSc Finance, Canterbury Christ Church University (2018)
- Previously: Data Analyst at Navitas

## What I Am Building Toward

- **Credit Risk Intelligence**: production ML for arrears prediction, survival analysis, portfolio forecasting, and anomaly detection in lending portfolios
- **Causal and XAI**: Difference-in-Differences, Propensity Score Matching, Bayesian experimentation, uplift modelling, and SHAP explainability in regulated financial services
- **MLOps**: MLflow experiment tracking and model registry, Evidently drift monitoring, champion or challenger versioning, FastAPI model serving, and Docker containerisation
- **Expert Python engineering**: typed functions, OOP, uv, logging, pytest, profiling, and optimisation
